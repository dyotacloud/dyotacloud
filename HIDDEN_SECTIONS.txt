1. SALESFORCE SECTION  (src/components/Salesforce.jsx)
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT SHOWS:
  - "Your Certified Salesforce Partner" heading with partner badges
    (Consulting Partner, ISV Partner, AppExchange Listed, Crest Partner)
  - 6 Salesforce service cards: Implementation, Apex & LWC Dev,
    API & Integration, Data Migration, Support & Maintenance, Customization
  - 5 Salesforce Cloud cards: Sales, Service, Marketing, Experience, Commerce
  - Benefits strip: Certified Experts / Agile Delivery / End-to-End Solutions

WHY COMMENTED:
  Temporarily hidden to keep the page focused. Re-enable when you want
  to highlight Salesforce capabilities as a dedicated section.

HOW TO RE-ENABLE:
  In src/App.jsx:
    Step 1 — Uncomment the import at the top:
      // import Salesforce from './components/Salesforce'
      →  import Salesforce from './components/Salesforce'

    Step 2 — Uncomment the JSX in <main>:
      {/* <Salesforce /> */}
      →  <Salesforce />

  Recommended placement: after <About /> and before <Services />

DATA SOURCE:
  src/data/siteContent.json  →  "salesforceServices" and "salesforceClouds" arrays
  src/assets/css/Salesforce.css
